From Reservation to Re-entry

The Experience

We've designed every moment of your NOVA journey — from your first phone call to the post-mission debrief — to be as extraordinary as the mission itself.

Your Journey

Step by Step

01
Step 01 — Discovery

Mission Consultation

Your journey begins with a private one-on-one consultation with a NOVA Mission Specialist. We learn your goals, answer every question, and help you choose the mission that's right for you. No pressure, just possibility.

📞
02
Step 02 — Preparation

Medical & Fitness Assessment

A comprehensive but non-intimidating health screening performed by our aerospace medicine team. We assess cardiovascular fitness, vestibular tolerance, and psychological readiness — then tailor your training accordingly.

🩹
03
Step 03 — Training

NOVA Astronaut Academy

Our world-class training campus in New Mexico prepares you for everything: centrifuge runs, microgravity water-tank simulations, emergency protocols, spacecraft systems, and comfort in your flight suit. Rigorous. Unforgettable.

🏋
04
Step 04 — Launch Week

Pre-Launch Rituals

Arrive at our spaceport for final mission rehearsals, suit fittings, and your ceremonial crew dinner. The night before launch, sleep under a desert sky full of the stars you'll soon be among.

🌞
05
Step 05 — The Mission

Launch & Beyond

Engines ignite. You are pressed into your seat at 3G as Earth falls away beneath you. Then silence — the most profound silence you've ever known — and the universe spreads out in every direction. This is what you came for.

🚀
06
Step 06 — Return

Re-entry & Landing

You return through a plasma sheath of re-entry, parachute down through clouds, and touch down safely. Your NOVA recovery team is there to greet you. Welcome back, astronaut.

🏝
NOVA Astronaut Academy

Your Training Program

Designed for civilians — not career astronauts. You don't need to be an athlete, engineer, or pilot. You just need to show up ready.

Days 1–3

Orientation & Theory

Spacecraft systems, orbital mechanics basics, emergency procedures, communication protocols, and your first zero-gravity simulation in our parabolic flight aircraft.

Days 4–10

Physical Conditioning

Centrifuge training, neutral buoyancy pool sessions, cardiovascular endurance work, and vestibular adaptation exercises guided by our aerospace physiology team.

Days 11–14

Crew Integration

Full mission simulations with your crew, emergency scenario drills, communication roleplay, and psychological resilience coaching.

Days 15–18

Vehicle Familiarization

Hands-on time inside the actual spacecraft — learning the cabin layout, window operation, restraint systems, suit donning/doffing, and personal equipment.

Days 19–21

Full Dress Rehearsal

End-to-end launch day simulation: wake-up protocols, breakfast, suit up, crew vehicle, launch site walkthrough, and full countdown procedure.

Launch Week

Pre-Mission Isolation

Light activity, final medical checks, rest, and psychological preparation. Private time with family before your launch day. This is your moment — we protect it.
