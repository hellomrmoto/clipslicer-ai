Our Story

A Mission That Started on the Ground

NOVA was founded in 2016 by aerospace engineer Elena Vasquez and propulsion physicist Dr. James Okafor, both former NASA contractors who shared a frustration: the technology to send civilians to space existed, but no one was building the experience around them.

After eight years of propulsion development, safety testing, and regulatory groundwork, NOVA launched its first crewed suborbital mission in 2022. It carried six passengers — a nurse, a photographer, a teacher, a retired pilot, a 72-year-old chef, and a marine biologist — and changed all of their lives forever.

Today, NOVA operates three distinct mission programs from our Mojave spaceport, with a second launch facility under construction in the Atacama Desert for southern hemisphere access. Our mission is simple: more people, more stars.

Our Journey

Milestones

  • 2016

    NOVA Founded

    Elena Vasquez and Dr. James Okafor incorporate NOVA Space Tourism, Inc. with a $40M seed round led by aerospace-focused venture capital.

  • 2018

    Constellation-1 Engine Test

    Successful static fire of the Constellation-1 liquid-fueled engine at the Mojave test site. 180 kN thrust, 98% reliability across 240 test burns.

  • 2020

    FAA Launch License Granted

    NOVA receives its Commercial Launch License from the FAA after 18 months of review — the first civilian-focused space tourism operator to receive Category III certification.

  • 2022

    First Crewed Mission

    NOVA Dawn — six civilian passengers, six minutes of weightlessness, zero incidents. A new chapter in human exploration begins.

  • 2024

    Orbital & Lunar Programs Launch

    Helix Station habitation module delivered to LEO. First Orbital Retreat passenger cohort completes their mission. First Lunar Passage crew returns safely after 12 days.

  • 2026

    2,300+ Passengers & Growing

    NOVA crosses 2,300 passengers flown. Atacama launch facility breaks ground. 2027 reservations now open.

Our Values

What We Stand For

01

Safety Without Compromise

Every design decision, every protocol, every materials choice is made with one question: "What if this fails?" Safety is not a feature. It's the foundation.

02

Radical Accessibility

We're building toward a future where space isn't just for the ultra-wealthy. Our NOVA Scholarship Program has sent 12 civilians to space who couldn't have afforded a ticket.

03

Environmental Stewardship

Every NOVA launch is fully carbon-offset. We invest 3% of revenue into sustainable propellant research and upper-atmosphere monitoring programs.

04

Transformative Intent

We measure success by whether our passengers come back changed. Not entertained — changed. The Overview Effect is real, and we believe it matters for Earth.
